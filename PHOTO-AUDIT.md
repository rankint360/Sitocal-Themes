# Theme photo and layout audit

The latest release covers all 29 themes. ClearFlow is v1.4.0 and the other themes are v1.2.0.

- Removed 444 visible Pexels credits. Source records remain with bundled assets.
- Added 1,294 images across 631 HTML pages, including three per homepage or ClearFlow preset and two per remaining page.
- Rechecked all local links and ZIP entry files. Chromium decoded the new gallery images on 286 sampled mobile and desktop loads without missing local requests, uncaught errors, or page-level horizontal overflow. ClearFlow passed its editor export and saved-project rebuild checks.

Earlier fixes included repaired images and script timing in DuneShield and Jetbrook, article navigation in SlabTrace and Tidewell, and horizontal overflow in the Pestward pair.
